Canon

An AI brand-governance platform that checks every draft, PDF and image against a brand's approved rules and facts before it's published, and proves what was approved.

  • Rule engine plus validated AI review: exact checks for prices, claims and disclaimers, and Gemini for tone, with every AI finding checked before it's shown
  • Computer vision on images: reads text in artwork, measures WCAG contrast and detects retired logos with multi-scale edge matching in NumPy
  • Signed approvals: HMAC-signed seals and a public page that shows whether content was approved, edited after approval, or never approved
  • Risk-based routing to fast, standard and legal review lanes, plus a governance dashboard
